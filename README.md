### ALGO-000178: Skolem problem at order five
- **Subfield:** Games, automata, and verification
- **Open:** Decide zero occurrence for all linear recurrence sequences of order five.
- **Status:** Orders up to four are decidable; order five is the first unresolved general order. Conditional transcendence-based results do not supply an unconditional decision algorithm.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** A sharply bounded test case for the general decidability problem.
- **Key works:** Lipton et al. (2022), *On the Skolem Problem and the Skolem Conjecture*, DOI: 10.1145/3531130.3533328; Bilu et al. (2022).
- **Related:** Transcendental number theory—effective Diophantine bounds.
